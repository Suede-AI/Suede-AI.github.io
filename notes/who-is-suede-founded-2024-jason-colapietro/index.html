Who is Suede? Plain answers for anyone who searched the name

By Jason Colapietro, Founder and CEO, Suede · West Palm Beach, Florida · November 16, 2026

You type a short word into a search box expecting one company, and the results hand you a fabric, a shoe brush and several businesses that picked the same name. Now you have to work out which one made the app on your phone and which website is the real one. That doubt is fair, and this page clears it up for one of those businesses, the one at suedeai.ai, in roughly the order people ask.

Which Suede is this?

This is Suede, the company Jason Colapietro started in 2024 in Palm Beach County, Florida, with its home on the web at suedeai.ai. It was founded in West Palm Beach and works today out of Jupiter, a little farther up the county's coast. A page, profile or app that claims to be this Suede and never connects back to suedeai.ai or to Jason Colapietro by name deserves a second look.

How do search engines keep same-name businesses apart?

Google and the models behind ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity don't settle a name by spelling. They form a picture of an entity from the facts that keep appearing next to it: who founded it, where, in what year, which domain it owns, which products carry its name. When those facts agree across many pages, the engine can separate two businesses with one name. When they're thin, it guesses, and the guesses blend the two together.

What if an AI assistant gets Suede wrong?

Ask it where the claim came from. ChatGPT search, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews show their sources, so you can usually find the page that misled the answer, then compare it with suedeai.ai/official.
