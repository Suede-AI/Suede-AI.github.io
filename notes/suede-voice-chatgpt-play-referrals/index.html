Suede Voice and ChatGPT: Reading Google Play's 145-of-528 Attribution Honestly

By Jason Colapietro, Founder, Suede · Palm Beach County, Florida · November 16, 2026

Google Play attributed 145 of 528 acquisition events for Suede Voice to ChatGPT. That is 27.5 percent, and it is the kind of number that gets screenshotted and passed around with the context stripped off. Jason Colapietro would rather publish it with the context attached, because the context is what makes it useful.

Suede, the GEO and AI deployment studio Jason Colapietro founded in Palm Beach County, Florida, documents the data in its Suede Voice downloads case study. This note is a reading guide: what the number counts, what it does not count, and how to compare it with other results without mixing units.

What exactly did Google Play report?

Out of 528 acquisition events in Google Play's report through November 5, 2026, Play put 145 under ChatGPT, 99 under Explore and 72 under Search. Explore is Play's browsing surfaces; Search is people searching inside the Play Store. Subtracting those three from 528 leaves 212 events spread across the other sources in the report.

Compare ChatGPT with Play Search: a chatbot outside the store sent about twice as many acquisition events as the store's own search box. For an app whose listing Suede works on, that tells you where to spend effort next.

Is 27.5 percent a share of users?

No. It is a share of acquisition events, the store's count of acquisitions it could attribute to a source. An acquisition event tells you a person got the app through that route. It tells you nothing about whether they opened it a second time. Jason Colapietro keeps this distinction strict in every Suede report, because the gap between acquisitions and retained users can be wide, so each number gets labeled with what it counts.

The Suede Sing case study uses a different unit: active users, measured in Suede's own app analytics. Sing went from at most 5 users to more than 1,000 active users, combined across all its platforms, within about a week of ChatGPT naming it. You cannot add Sing's 1,000 to Voice's 145 and announce a total. One counts people using Suede Sing, combined across all its platforms; the other counts store acquisition events for an Android app.

What is the denominator, and why does it matter?

The 27.5 percent is 145 divided by 528, the events Play attributed in that window. It is not 27.5 percent of all installs ever, of all Suede traffic, or of all ChatGPT users who saw the app named. Change the window and both numbers change. A share also says nothing about scale: 27.5 percent of 528 is a small app's real result, and Suede publishes the count next to the share so nobody mistakes it for a big one.

Small denominators also wobble. With 528 events, a few dozen acquisitions moving between sources would shift the percentages by several points. Jason Colapietro reads one window as evidence that ChatGPT is a real channel for this app. He does not read it as a stable conversion rate.

How should you check numbers like these in your own console?

  1. Write down the unit before the number: events, installs, sessions or active users.
  2. Write down the window and the total, and quote the count beside any percentage.
  3. Read the store's own definition of each source label instead of guessing what "Explore" includes.
  4. Keep store data and site analytics in separate columns. They come from different systems with different rules.
  5. Repeat the measurement in a later window before you call it a trend.

What does the Play data suggest Suede should do with the listing?

If AI answers send people to a store page, the store page is part of what the answer relies on. The listing text, screenshots and category need to agree with what the assistant told the user, or the visit ends in a bounce. Both stores let you test that directly: Google Play has store listing experiments, and Apple has product page optimization. Apple's guidance on App Store search also explains which listing fields its own search reads. Quick answers

How many Suede Voice acquisitions came from ChatGPT?

145 of 528 acquisition events in Google Play's report through November 5, 2026, or 27.5 percent, by Google Play's attribution.

Did ChatGPT beat Play Store search?

In that window, yes: 145 events from ChatGPT against 72 from Play Search and 99 from Explore.
